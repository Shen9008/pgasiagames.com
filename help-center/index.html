Getting started at PGAsia (pgasiagames.com) is designed to be fast and straightforward, but there are a few important steps every new player should understand before making their first deposit.

Why You Should Complete KYC Early

KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is a legal requirement under our licensing framework. It confirms that you are who you say you are, that you are of legal gambling age in your jurisdiction, and that your payment details match your identity. While you can typically browse and play before KYC, the process blocks large withdrawals until it is complete. Starting your KYC at registration rather than when you first request a withdrawal avoids delays at the most frustrating possible moment—when you want to cash out a win. Documents required are typically: a government-issued photo ID (passport, national identity card, or driving licence) and a proof of address dated within the last 90 days (utility bill, bank statement, or official government letter). Ensure documents are in focus, uncropped, and all four corners are visible.

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

PGAsia strongly recommends enabling 2FA on your account. With 2FA active, logging in requires both your password and a time-based one-time code from an authenticator app (e.g. Google Authenticator, Authy) or an SMS code to your registered phone number. Even if your password is compromised, 2FA prevents unauthorised access. To enable: go to Account Settings → Security → Two-Factor Authentication. If you lose access to your 2FA device, contact support from your registered email with your identity documents to initiate recovery.
