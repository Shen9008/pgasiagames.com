Warning Signs of Problem Gambling

Problem gambling affects people regardless of income, education, or nationality. Cultural stigma in some Asian communities delays help-seeking—recognising patterns early reduces long-term damage. Consider a pause and professional conversation if you notice:

  • Spending more time or money than planned, repeatedly breaking self-set limits
  • Chasing losses with larger deposits or higher stakes
  • Borrowing money, selling assets, or using credit to gamble
  • Neglecting work, studies, family, or sleep because of play
  • Hiding gambling from partners or friends; lying about losses
  • Feeling irritable, anxious, or restless when not gambling
  • Using gambling to escape stress, grief, or depression
  • Failed attempts to cut back despite wanting to stop

One or two occasional behaviours do not automatically indicate disorder—but patterns that persist across weeks deserve attention. Free confidential screening tools exist through organisations linked below; scores are starting points for conversation, not diagnoses.
