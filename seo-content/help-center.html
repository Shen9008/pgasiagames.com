KYC Document Guide: Getting Verified First Time

Identity verification protects your winnings. Submitting correct documents the first time saves days of back-and-forth.

Acceptable Documents

  • Photo ID: Passport, national ID card, or driving licence. All four corners visible, no glare, not expired.
  • Proof of address: Utility bill, bank statement, or government letter dated within three months. Must show your full name and address matching registration.
  • Payment proof (if requested): Redacted screenshot showing method and name—hide full card numbers except last four digits.

Common Rejection Reasons

  1. Blurry photo or cropped edges on ID.
  2. Name mismatch between ID and account profile.
  3. Address document older than three months.
  4. Screenshot of a screenshot—submit original camera photo or scan.
  5. Using someone else's payment method or document.

Upload through the verification portal inside your account. Our compliance team typically responds within 24–48 hours. For legal context on why KYC exists, see About Us.
