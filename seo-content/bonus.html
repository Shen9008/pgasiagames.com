Wagering Requirements Explained

Wagering requirements—also called turnover, playthrough, or rollover—define how much you must bet before bonus funds or winnings tied to them become withdrawable cash. This is the single most important concept to understand before accepting any PGAsia promotion.

Suppose you receive a MYR 200 bonus with a 30× wagering requirement on the bonus amount only. You would need to place MYR 6,000 in total qualifying bets (200 × 30) before the bonus balance converts to withdrawable funds. If the requirement applies to deposit plus bonus, the calculation uses the combined amount—making the target significantly higher. Always check which base the multiplier uses.
