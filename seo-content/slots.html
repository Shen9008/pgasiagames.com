RTP and Volatility: Honest Expectations

Return to Player (RTP) is the percentage of total wagers a game is designed to return over millions of spins—typically between 94% and 97% on mainstream titles, with some exceptions higher or lower. Volatility (or variance) describes how those returns are distributed: low volatility smooths the ride; high volatility increases swing size. PGAsia lists both where the provider supplies data; some games offer multiple RTP configurations—always confirm which version loaded in your session.
