Baccarat — The Cornerstone of Asian Live Casino

Baccarat is the single most-played casino card game in Asia, and it dominates the PGAsia live lobby accordingly. The rules are straightforward: two hands (Player and Banker) are dealt, and you bet on which will score closest to nine—or bet on a Tie. The Banker hand carries a marginally lower house edge (around 1.06%) compared to Player (1.24%) when factoring in the standard 5% commission on Banker wins. Tie bets, while paying 8:1 or 9:1, carry an edge above 14%—use them sparingly or not at all if your focus is bankroll longevity.

The PGAsia Baccarat tables include road display options (Big Road, Big Eye Boy, Small Road, Cockroach Road) familiar to experienced Baccarat players. These road patterns track historical outcomes, but they do not predict the future—each hand is independent. They are useful for tracking trends and are part of the authentic Baccarat culture. Teen Patti — India's Favourite Card Game Goes Live

Teen Patti (literally "Three Cards" in Hindi) is the most searched live casino game among Indian players at PGAsia. Comparable to three-card poker, players and the dealer are each dealt three cards. The player bets blind (without seeing their hand) or seen (after viewing), and the goal is to have a better three-card hand than the dealer. Ranking from highest: trail (three of a kind) → pure sequence (straight flush) → sequence (straight) → colour (flush) → pair → high card. Side bets on pair-plus and bonus outcomes are common—check the payout table and house edge before placing them.

Andar Bahar — Fast-Paced and Easy to Learn

Andar Bahar is one of the simplest live casino games available at PGAsia. A single card (the "joker") is dealt face up. Cards are then dealt alternately to the Andar (inside) and Bahar (outside) piles until a card matching the joker's value appears. You bet on which pile will receive the matching card first. The house edge is approximately 2.15% on Andar and 3% on Bahar for the first deal. Subsequent deals shift the probability slightly. The game's appeal lies in its speed—a round can conclude in under a minute—and its cultural familiarity for South Asian players.

Dragon Tiger — Two Cards, One Decision

Dragon Tiger is arguably the fastest live casino game in the PGAsia lobby. One card is dealt to Dragon, one to Tiger—the higher card wins. You bet on Dragon, Tiger, or Tie. The standard bet carries a house edge of around 3.73%, while the Tie bet (paying 8:1) has an edge above 32%. Dragon Tiger is popular as a pace-of-play game—rounds end in seconds. It is a common entry point for players new to live casino, and it suits short, high-tempo sessions.

Sic Bo — Dice Game with Asian Heritage

Sic Bo is a dice game with origins in ancient China, now available in HD at PGAsia live tables. Three dice are shaken in a chest or electronic shaker; players bet on outcomes before the reveal.
